What's the difference between hearing and listening?
Hearing is physical: sound reaches your ears. Listening is active: you pay attention, interpret and respond. Your notes put it as "hearing is passive, listening is deliberate."

For the exam, remember the 4 stages of listening: receiving, understanding, evaluating, responding.
